Hummus

This delicious creamy hummus is best served with a pita bread.

Ingredients

  • Chickpeas in a can
  • Tahini
  • Lemon juice
  • Garlic
  • Pita breads to serve
  • Olive oil

Instructions

  1. Blend chickpeas, tahini, garlic with some water in a food processor. Add salt to taste.
  2. Serve with warm flatbreads, topped with olive oil.
